# Google Business Profile — setup guide (Multi-Assainissement)

Configure GBP **without keyword stuffing the business name**.

## Important: English editor (Tunisia)

For many listings in Tunisia, **the whole Business Profile dashboard is in English**:

- Menus: **Edit profile**, **Business category**, **Services**, **Description**, **Opening hours**, **Service area**, **Add photos**, **Ask for reviews**
- **Category autocomplete** is in English — you must **click a suggestion**; pasted text shows *We didn't understand your category…*

Use **English** for **categories** and **predefined service titles** (Google’s list). Write **descriptions in French** for Tunisia — clients and hotel staff mostly search in French (*désinsectisation Djerba*, *cafards*, *dératisation*). Descriptions help readers understand you immediately; they are not a guaranteed ranking lever. Keep sentences natural (no keyword lists). Phone is already in **Phone** — service lines can end with « Devis sur WhatsApp ».

---

## Before you start (5 minutes)

1. Open [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) (or Google Maps → your profile → **Edit profile**).
2. Sign in with the account that **manages** Multi-Assainissement.
3. Have ready: **26 848 831** / **+216 268 488 31**, Mezraya / Djerba address or **Service area**, 3–5 photos (logo, van, team).

---

## Step 1 — Business name

1. **Edit profile** → **Business name**
2. Enter exactly: **`Multi-Assainissement`** (or **`Multi-Assainissement SARL`** if that matches invoices and the van)
3. Do **not** add Djerba, pest control keywords, etc.
4. **Save**

---

## Step 2 — Primary category

1. **Edit profile** → **Business category** → **Primary category**
2. Clear the field, type **`pest`**
3. **Click** from the list: **Pest control service**
4. No red error under the field → **Save**

*(Do not type French category names unless the dropdown shows them and you click one.)*

---

## Step 3 — Additional categories

**Business category** → **Add another category**

| Type | Click this suggestion |
|------|------------------------|
| `sanit` | **Sanitation service** |
| `animal` | **Animal control and welfare service** |

In Tunisia there is often **no** separate **Animal control service** — use **Animal control and welfare service** if you sell stray cat capture.

**Do not add:** Sanitary inspection, Environmental attorney, Environmental Service, Pool cleaning service (until piscine is live), or any line with a red category error.

**Recommended today**

1. **Primary:** Pest control service  
2. **Additional:** Sanitation service  
3. **Additional:** Animal control and welfare service *(optional)*

No separate Google category for “rodent control” or “insect control” — they stay under **Pest control service**.

---

## Step 4 — Services

**Edit profile** → **Services**

Under **Pest control service** (Primary category), Google shows a **fixed English list**. Tap each line → add **price** (optional) and **description** (optional) → save.

### Turn ON these predefined services (core business)

| Tap this service | Why |
|------------------|-----|
| **Cockroach extermination** | Main offer (blattes / décafarisation) |
| **Rodent extermination** | Dératisation |
| **Bed bug extermination** | Hôtels / locations |
| **Bug and insect extermination** | General désinsectisation |

### Add only if you really do them

| Service | When |
|---------|------|
| **Ant extermination** | If you treat ants on request |
| **Flea and mite extermination** | If offered |
| **Hornet and wasp extermination** | If offered (guêpes/frelons) |
| **Spider extermination** | Usually skip unless common for you |

Do **not** enable every line “to look complete” — only real services.

### Custom services (**+ Add more services**)

Google has **no** preset for Legionella or pool cleaning. Use **+ Add more services** under **Pest control service** (or under **Sanitation service** for Legionella if that fits better):

| Custom name (English recommended) | Notes |
|-----------------------------------|--------|
| **Legionella prevention and control** | Lutte anti-légionellose |
| **Stray cat capture** | Or keep **Capturation des chats** if you already added it (French custom names are OK here) |
| **Mosquito treatment** | Or **Traitement anti-moustiques** if already added |
| **Pool cleaning** | Only when live — not “coming soon” |

**Photos** → **Add photos**: logo, van/team, 1–2 job photos (no macro pests), **Cover** photo.

**Ask for reviews** → share link with 2–3 hotel contacts (WhatsApp). Ask for **French** reviews that mention the **service** and **Djerba** — steady flow beats a single spike.

## Changing editor language (optional)

Google Account → **Language** → **Français** may show some French category labels; **categories and services in this guide remain valid**. If French suggestions appear, you can use **Service d'extermination des nuisibles** instead of **Pest control service** — still **click** the suggestion, never paste free text.
