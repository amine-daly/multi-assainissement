## 6. What this file says about ranking first

The measurable market in this location set is a few dozen searches a month per theme, shared by many wordings, with almost no advertisers bidding (competition Low or unknown, no bids recorded). That is a winnable local market. It is not a market that grows because more URLs are created.

Order of work:

1. Keep one désinsectisation URL and one dératisation URL. Done.
2. Use the titles and the three H2s in section 5. Done on the site.
3. On the Business Profile, fix opening hours, keep the legal name, and add the four services above.
4. Ask for reviews that mention the job and Djerba. The keyword file cannot move the map pack. Reviews can.
5. In spring, promote cafards again. Through winter, punaises de lit and dératisation are the flat terms.
6. Arabic titles now follow section 7. French titles stay as in section 5.
